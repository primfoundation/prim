# Foundation program charter

Status: implementation direction authorized September 6, 2026. Public standards changes remain reviewable drafts until their gates pass.

## Mission

Make digital filing cabinets free for billions of people. A Prim represents something meaningful in the world, not merely a replacement document format. People and agents should retain, understand, correct, continue, and share durable work without depending on the model, application, vendor, repository, or maintainer that produced it.

The system should increasingly explain what exists, how it connects, what is claimed and why, what happened, what is owed or intended, what was decided, what is running, what is possible, and what is authorized. Unknown, contradictory, stale, inaccessible, and deliberately unfiled information remain distinct.

## Preserve these boundaries

The work product is separate from intelligence, execution, interfaces, and exports. Foundation stewardship is separate from Eidos or any other commercial implementation. Original evidence is not the thing it describes; a claim is not automatically true. Human authority, software capability, and knowledge are distinct.

The September 2 working constitution's Entity, Activity, Claim, and Context remain a small provisional semantic substrate. They do not silently replace the existing category's packaging/tool primitives or become four mandatory tables. Reconciliation requires examples and a decision. Relationship, Role, State, Commitment, Intention, Decision, Rule, Capability, Authority, and Evidence are useful semantics, not automatic new file types.

A profile's identity is independent of repository location. One repository may contain many publishable profiles. External publishers do not need a Foundation repository. The central directory discovers public definitions; it does not own private instances. Local/offline use and private definitions are legitimate first-class cases.

Definitions, structural conformance, semantic review, publisher identity, security review, and factual truth must not collapse into one badge. Existing files keep their documented interpretation until an explicit, tested migration. A convenient rename or alias is not a compatibility adapter.

No giant universal schema, mandatory RDF rewrite, premature hundreds of types, or obligation to restructure every original artifact. No fixed UI embedded in the format, but usable reference hosts are real obligations. People should not need ontology vocabulary, GitHub, or a terminal to use their information.

## Broad coverage without owning every implementation

The Foundation stewards category contracts, publication, conformance, governance, and a small reference profile set. Reference products prove usability. Ecosystem publishers extend the system independently. Each mission obligation needs an architectural home and an accountable delivery path, not necessarily Foundation-owned application code.

The coverage map includes governance; core contracts; publication; discovery; Research; relationships/composition; developer/agent tools; human products; ingestion; authority; security/privacy; whole-life coverage; interoperability; release/migration; operations/sustainability; and adoption/community. The plan decomposes these into acceptance-testable outcomes.

Research is first. Person, receipt, contract, project, decision, and system remain deliberately different reference cases testing whether abstractions generalize. Whole-life diagnostics, extraction contracts, and Explorer/World experiences follow representation tests, rather than becoming premature data dumping.

## Cross-cutting acceptance

Ordinary recipients can understand a Prim without the original app. Offline operation and unavailable references produce honest partial states. Corrections, concurrent changes, supersession, redaction, deletion, retention, and legal holds have explicit, policy-governed behavior; append-only logs alone do not solve privacy. Recursive ingestion is bounded and resumable. Untrusted definitions never grant execution authority. Private records do not leak through discovery or telemetry.

Cost, accessibility, intermittent connectivity, resource-limited devices, different languages/locales, and different household/organizational arrangements are design constraints. The Foundation represents commitments and authority; it is not automatically authorized to make payments, give regulated advice, or act on a person's behalf.

Free/open core does not imply an unbudgeted promise to subsidize unlimited hosted compute or storage. Hosting, support, licensing changes, trademarks, legal structure, and sustainability commitments require named human decisions and appropriate review.

## Completion standard

Completion is against an explicit release scope: verified end-to-end outcomes, migrations, operational readiness, contributor usability, and actual-user feedback. A plan is not implementation; code is not deployment; deployment is not usefulness. New discoveries extend the map without quietly deleting old obligations.

This document consolidates the September 2 architecture discussion and September 6 Research/profile direction. It is a sanitized synthesis, not publication of the underlying private conversation or retrospective approval of every earlier proposal.
